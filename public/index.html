Our Story

The Supra Never Ends

In Georgia, a meal is not served — it is led. A tamada, the toastmaster, calls each round: to peace, to ancestors, to the guests at the table. Gyenos was built around that same table.

Our wine is fermented the old way, in qvevri — clay vessels buried in the earth, a method practiced here for over eight thousand years. Everything else follows that same patience: bread pulled from a clay tone oven, dumplings folded by hand, meat left to marinate overnight before it meets the fire.
